This is where I strongly suspect your scheduler may be losing throughput.

For each SKU:

Plant:

If the plant uses 7 and your optimizer uses 4, you found a major problem.

4. Check whether you are over-constraining

This is one of the first things I'd investigate in your code.

Your logic may be doing:

Allowable Building
AND
Allowable Curing
AND
Mould
AND
Inch lock
AND
Same press
AND
Same campaign
AND
Lot size
AND
Changeover
AND
Buffer

Individually these rules may be correct.

But their combination may be unnecessarily restrictive.

Plant may be using:

"If feasible, run it."

Your engine may be saying:

"Only run it if every preferred condition is simultaneously satisfied."

That can easily create your 90% ceiling.

6. Check GT starvation

Don't only look at total Building production.

Look at minute-by-minute / shift-by-shift curing starvation.

For every curing press:

Available time
- Running time
- Changeover
- Idle because GT unavailable
- Idle because mould unavailable
- Idle because wrong SKU

Especially:

How many hours did my curing presses sit idle while Building had capacity?

That tells you whether your Building→Curing synchronization is wrong.

7. Check GT inventory

This is VERY important given your previous issue.

Calculate:

GT Produced
GT Consumed by Curing
GT Closing Inventory

If your engine gives:

Building = 100,000
Curing   = 90,000
GT Inv   = +10,000

then your problem isn't Building capacity.

Your scheduler is producing 10,000 tyres that curing cannot consume.

Your target should be closer to:

Building → just enough ahead of Curing
Curing   → maximum feasible consumption
GT Inv   → controlled buffer
8. Do a "constraint kill test"

This is probably the fastest way to locate the bad code.

Run your scheduler multiple times:

Run 1 — Current engine
Production = 90%
Run 2 — Remove changeover optimization

If:

90% → 94%

Changeover logic is costing you 4%.

Run 3 — Relax lot-size restriction
90% → 96%

Lot sizing is costing you 6%.

Run 4 — Relax inch-lock constraint
90% → 98%

Now you know inch-lock is responsible for ~8%.

Run 5 — Remove mould restriction temporarily

etc.

This is basically a constraint sensitivity analysis.
